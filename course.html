What is an ATS?

An Applicant Tracking System is software companies use to receive, store and search CVs. When you apply, it parses your CV into pieces (name, jobs, dates, skills), stores them, and lets a recruiter search or rank them. Then a person reviews the best matches.

Most systems do not reject you automatically. The real danger is a CV the software cannot read: your details land in the wrong place or go missing, and recruiters never find you. So a good CV is readable and relevant.

Practice: copy the job post you want into a document. You will use it in lesson 4.

3

CV structure

  1. Contact details: name, phone (+234...), professional email, city and state, LinkedIn (optional)
  2. Professional Summary: 2 to 4 lines
  3. Skills: relevant, and only those you can defend
  4. Work Experience: newest first
  5. Education: degree, school, year
  6. Certifications and optional Projects or Volunteering
Summary formula: [Level or title] with [experience] in [field]. Skilled in [3 skills from the job post]. [What you will do for the employer.]
Hardworking person looking for a good company to grow.
Organised Operations Coordinator with 2 years in scheduling, client communication and records. Skilled in Excel and appointment coordination.

Leave out date of birth, marital status, religion, state of origin and "references available on request".

4

Keywords

Keywords are the words in the job post: titles, skills, tools and duties. Use their wording where it is true for you.

  1. Highlight every skill, tool and duty in the job post.
  2. Words repeated more than once matter most.
  3. Make two lists: what you truly have, and what you lack (never lie).
  4. Place the first list in your Summary, Skills and bullets.
From this job post: Operations Coordinator, operations support, scheduling and confirming appointments, client service, coordination with officers.

Do not stuff keywords or hide white text. Use each one naturally.

5

Strong bullet points

Formula: action verb + what you did + how + result (with a number).

WeakStrong
Responsible for answering callsHandled 40+ client calls daily, resolving 90% of enquiries on first contact
Did data entryEntered and verified 200+ customer records weekly in Excel with 99% accuracy
Helped organise eventsCoordinated logistics for a 150-guest event, on schedule and 10% under budget

Start with a past-tense verb (Coordinated, Scheduled, Reduced, Led). No "I" or "my". No numbers? Estimate honestly ("over 20"). Never invent figures.

Tailoring and final checks

The 15-minute routine for every application

  1. Read the job post twice and highlight 8 to 10 keywords.
  2. Mirror the job title and 3 key skills in your Summary.
  3. Put the most relevant skills first.
  4. Adjust 2 or 3 bullets to match the main duties.
  5. Save a new copy as Firstname_Lastname_CV.
  6. Spell-check, then read it aloud.
Copy-paste test: select all in your CV, paste it into Notepad. If the order is logical and your name, phone and email are there, the software can read it.
